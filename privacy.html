Privacy Policy

Last updated:

Madhu's Tours & Travels ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you use this website or contact us, and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Contact details you share with us — name, phone number, email, and travel dates — when you submit an enquiry form or message us on WhatsApp.
  • Basic usage data (such as pages visited) collected automatically via Google Analytics, to help us understand how the site is used.

How we use your information

  • To respond to booking enquiries and provide the travel services you request.
  • To contact you about your booking, including confirmations and updates.
  • To improve this website and our services.

Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share necessary booking details (such as your name and travel date) with third-party service providers — for example, bus operators, airlines, or hotels — solely to fulfil your booking.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete any personal information we hold about you, or ask what information we have, by emailing madhusaipanasa47@gmail.com.
