10 Best Places to Visit in and Around Rajahmundry

Rajahmundry sits right on the Godavari River, and that single fact shapes almost everything worth seeing here — from riverfront ghats to forested hills a couple of hours out of town. Here's our local list, roughly in the order most visitors see them.

1. Godavari River Ghats & Bridges

Start in town — the riverfront ghats and the view of the Godavari road and rail bridges are worth an early morning or sunset walk, especially if you're arriving the night before a longer trip.

2. Papikondalu Hills

The signature trip from Rajahmundry — a river cruise through a forested gorge that's often compared to a mini Amazon. See our full Papikondalu travel guide for planning details, or go straight to package pricing.

3. Maredumilli

A couple of hours from Rajahmundry, Maredumilli is thick reserve forest with waterfalls, bamboo groves and a noticeably cooler climate — a good add-on if your trip has a spare day.

4. Draksharamam

One of the Pancharama Kshetras, home to the ancient Bhimeswara Swamy temple — a manageable half-day trip from Rajahmundry, often combined with Annavaram.

5. Annavaram

The Satyanarayana Swamy temple atop Ratnagiri hill is one of the most-visited darshan spots in the region and pairs naturally with Draksharamam on the same day out.

6. Kotipalli & the Godavari Delta

If you want a quieter riverside stop without a full cruise, the delta stretches near Kotipalli offer a slower look at how the Godavari splits before reaching the coast.

7. Bhadrachalam

A longer trip (around 4–5 hours by road) to the revered Sri Seetharamachandraswamy temple — read our dedicated Rajahmundry to Bhadrachalam guide before you go.

8. Polavaram Project

The large Godavari barrage project makes for an interesting stop if you're curious about the scale of engineering along this stretch of the river — often included on our Papikondalu route.

9. Kadiyam Nurseries

A cluster of plant nurseries just outside Rajahmundry, popular with garden enthusiasts and a pleasant, unhurried stop if you're not in a rush.

10. Rajahmundry's local markets

Before you leave, the town's markets are worth a walk for local produce and handicrafts — a good way to spend an evening between longer day trips.
