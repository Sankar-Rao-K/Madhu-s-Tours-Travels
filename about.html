Our story

Started with one car. Built on one promise.

What began as a single-cab local taxi service in Rajahmundry has grown into a full travel desk — because every customer asked for the same thing: a driver and a booking they could trust without chasing follow-ups.

Today we coordinate car rentals, bus, train and flight ticket bookings, Papikondalu river cruises, and devotional yatra packages, all managed by the same team that answers your WhatsApp message. No call centres, no middlemen — just people in Rajahmundry who know the roads, the boats and the routes.
