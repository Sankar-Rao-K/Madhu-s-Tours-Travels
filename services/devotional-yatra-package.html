About this service

Pilgrimage trips, planned with care

The Godavari delta is home to some of Andhra Pradesh's most revered temples — Annavaram's Satyanarayana Swamy temple, Draksharamam's Bhimeswara temple, and Bhadrachalam's Sri Seetharamachandraswamy temple among them. We plan devotional yatras that get pilgrims of every age there comfortably, with darshan timings, transport and meals arranged in advance.

Travelling as a family, a temple group, or organising a senior citizens' trip? We tailor the vehicle, pace and stops to the group. We also run two fixed-departure group yatras — a 14-day Kashi Maha Yatra and a 6-day Tirupati & Arunachalam yatra — detailed below.

  • Darshan & timing planning
  • Comfortable group transport
  • Meals & rest stops arranged
  • Senior-citizen friendly pace
